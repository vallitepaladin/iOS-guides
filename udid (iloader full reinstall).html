SideStore UDID Fix for iOS 26.4+

Those who are having issues with iLoader and iOS 26.4+ regarding SideStore, here is a small tutorial on how to fix your pairing file.

Explanation

The reason your pairing file is broken is due to a borked lockdown pairingfile from the old versions of iLoader. In the newer versions of iLoader a new method of pairing was implemented, RPPairing. It is a more stable method of pairing compared to lockdown, and can never expire. The pairing also stays active when switching to cellular.

Guide

  1. If you had iLoader installed before RPPairing was implemented, delete iLoader and any files related to it off your computer first before following the rest of the guide.
  2. First, make sure you have the latest version of iLoader installed (2.2.5 or newer).
  3. If you already have the latest iLoader installed, make sure you are logged in to your Apple ID. Scroll down and click "Delete Stored Pairing">".

    This will remove any existing pairing file, but you will have to pair your phone again. This will get rid of the cached lockdown pairing.
  4. Go back to iLoader, pair your phone again, and install SideStore.
  5. Once SideStore is installed, test an app to see if it installs correctly. If it does, your pairing file is fixed and you can use SideStore as normal. If need be, change the anisette server to 'Macley'.

Extra Steps if the steps above do not work

  1. Open LocalDevVPN, find the Tunnel IP address.
  2. Go to the SideStore settings, find VPN Configuration, and set the Device IP to the Tunnel IP address you found in LocalDevVPN.
  3. Refresh your apps like normal.

Extra Steps if the steps above still do not work

  1. Go to Settings, click General, scroll down to "VPN and Device Management", click your email under "Developer App", and click "Delete Apps".

  2. Go back to iLoader, pair your phone again, and install SideStore.
